16 November 2026 - Medic qualification

Changed
- Exact vitals, diagnosis graphs and pinned patient monitoring now also unlock for characters with the vanilla medic qualification: the MEDIC group role (including a role set via NIRE Advanced GM) or the ROLE_MEDIC character label. A carried medical kit still works as before.

Previous update - 7 November 2026

Added
- Pin up to three patients and keep their pulse, blood pressure and ACE vital state visible while AMI is closed or you treat someone else.
- Each pinned patient requires a medical kit in your inventory and must remain within 10 metres.
- Critical patients receive a pulsing red panel, a bright red border and a dedicated triple-beep alarm. The alarm repeats every 5 seconds for critical/resuscitation states and every 2 seconds during cardiac arrest. Alarms can be muted per patient.
- Pinned patients appear first in the patient list, with stable ordering and bold names.
- Hover a blocked action or select it with a controller to see why it is unavailable. Explanations cover treatment, medication, Actions and monitor controls.

Changed
- Compact pinned panels with rounded corners and a 75% opaque black background.
- Remaining panels move left to close gaps; new patients are added on the right. Readings and alarm settings are retained.
- All new interface text is available in all 13 supported Reforger languages.

Fixed
- Reforger 1.9 issues affecting patient names, line breaks and monitoring buttons.
- ACE medical-kit detection for detailed vital readings and diagnosis graphs.
- Patient death or exceeding the monitoring range now removes only the affected panel. Other patients remain monitored.
- Monitor callback errors during pinning and cleanup.
- Missing or outdated readings are shown as unavailable and do not trigger alarms.

ACE remains responsible for medical states, treatment effects and medical replication.
