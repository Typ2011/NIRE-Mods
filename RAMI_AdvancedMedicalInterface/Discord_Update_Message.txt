# Advanced Medical Interface Update — 23 August 2026

AMI received a major patient-selection, vehicle-treatment, and interface update.

## Patient Selection
- Open a dedicated patient list containing yourself, patients within **5 m**, and every occupant of your vehicle.
- Select a patient directly by name and choose **TREAT**.
- Treat occupants while driving, riding, or operating a turret.
- Unload unconscious occupants through the native **UNLOAD FROM VEHICLE** action.
- See bleeding, unconscious, and deceased states directly in the patient list.

## Medical Information
- Exact vitals and Diagnose graphs now require a **medical kit**. Without one, AMI shows qualitative readings.
- Diagnose readings appear immediately after starting a monitor.
- Saline now uses one inventory-aware menu ordered **500 ml, vanilla 750 ml, 1000 ml, and 1500 ml**.
- Active saline appears on both Treatment and Medication with a bright-yellow body indicator.
- Vitals and active medications use separate compact cards; long medication lists support scrolling.

## Interface Improvements
- Added compact ACE3 medical icons to all page tabs.
- Added clear active-page and selected-body-zone outlines without hiding medical-state colors.
- Improved patient-list, button, hover, selection, and card styling throughout AMI.
- Actions such as CPR, carry, drag, positioning, vehicle loading, and unloading keep AMI open.
- Standardized remaining visible interface text to English.
- Fixed controller navigation from a selected patient to **TREAT**, then to enabled **UNLOAD FROM VEHICLE** or **CLOSE**.

## Fixes
- Fixed saline-menu null pointers during page setup.
- Fixed casualty unloading from the wrong vehicle compartment.

> AMI development is feature-complete for this phase. Final visual checks, dedicated-server regression testing, localization, and Workshop packaging remain pending.
