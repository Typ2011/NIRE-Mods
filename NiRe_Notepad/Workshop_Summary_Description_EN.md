# NiRe Notepad – Workshop EN

## Summary

NiRe Notepad adds a persistent field notebook to Arma Reforger, available during gameplay and directly on the map. Manage general notes, Fire Missions, Supply Requests, and Evacs with templates plus free notes. Terrain Baptism provides pen and eraser tools. Personal notes remain local; Logistics requests synchronize for authorized players. Fully localized in all 13 supported languages.

## Description

NiRe Notepad gives soldiers, JTACs, forward observers, logisticians, and medics a compact field notebook for ongoing operations.

Features:

- Open and close it with a rebindable action; default key: F6
- Available during gameplay and on the open map
- The map remains movable and zoomable outside the notepad
- Six sections: General, CAS, Artillery, Logistics, MEDEVAC, and Terrain Baptism
- Named, reusable, and deletable entries for CAS, Artillery, and MEDEVAC plus synchronized Supply Requests
- Fixed 5-/9-liners for CAS and MEDEVAC plus fixed 5-/7-liners for Artillery
- Editable prompt/answer fields for every template point and ten individual rows for Free Text
- Structured Logistics requests with material, quantity, pickup/delivery, coordinate, four NOTE rows, and status
- Persistent Terrain Baptism with colors, pen, eraser, and clear all
- Dedicated journals for Fire Missions, Supply Requests, and Evacs
- Separate requester and logistician permissions with chat status updates
- Immediate local saving of personal notes with additional temporary-file and backup recovery
- Notes survive disconnects, reconnects, crashes, and restarts
- Complete localization for all 13 languages supported by Arma Reforger

Controls:

- F6 opens or closes the notepad by default.
- Rebind it under Settings → Controls → NiRe Notepad.
- ESC closes the notepad first.
- Clicking outside the writing area returns focus to the game or map.

Notes:

- Personal notes are stored locally. Logistics requests are synchronized server-authoritatively with authorized faction members.
- The mod has no ACE runtime dependency. Optional server rules can restrict Logistics crate prefabs and contents.
- Templates are fixed working aids. Verify them against your unit's SOPs before operational use.
